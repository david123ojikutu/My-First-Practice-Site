# The Development of Art Hub
In developing this website, I began with what I remember as the basic constituents of a modern website, commonly known as a "boilerplate". This included \<html lang="en">\</html>, \<head>\</head>, \<body>\</body>, and \<footer>\</footer>. Then I added basic metadata in the \<head>\</head> element: \<meta charset="UTF-8">, \<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">, and the \<title>\</title> element. The first sets the character encoding of the website to UTF-8, the second modifies the digital canvas of the website to automatically fit the proportions of any modern device's screen, and the title just tells browsers and search engine crawlers the title.
