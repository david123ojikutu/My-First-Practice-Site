Hear from Alex

On the Art of Cooking

Cooking isn’t just about following a recipe — it’s about expression, connection, and passion. Even celebrities understand the human side of food. As Pitt said:

“I’ve been no stranger to change, but I think there’s something comforting about the rituals around food — the preparation, the sharing, the celebration that brings people together.”

– Brad Pitt, Interview with Vanity Fair

This reminds us that cooking can be an act of creativity and togetherness that transcends cultures. As the French say, « La cuisine, c’est l’amour rendu visible. » It’s a timeless truth: food is one of the most universal languages, capable of speaking to hearts everywhere.

Cooking Terms Glossary

Sauté
To cook food quickly in a small amount of oil or butter over high heat.
Marinate
To soak foods in a seasoned liquid before cooking to add flavor.
Blanch
To briefly boil food and then plunge it into ice water to stop cooking.

Cultural Connections through Cuisine

Food traditions often carry historical depth and a touch of science. For instance, precise temperatures like 100°C turn water into steam for perfect rice, while baking bread requires yeast activity at around 40°C. Some ancient dishes have changed over time — once rare ingredients are now forbidden replaced with sustainable alternatives.

In Japan, the savory pancake known as お好み焼き(Okonomiyaki) represents creativity and comfort. In France, the phrase « Bon goût, bon cœur » summarizes their belief that good taste reflects a good heart.
